# KMUTT Quantum Hardware Testbed (ZCU104)

Hardware acceleration and embedded testbed for quantum research projects at King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT), centered around the AMD Zynq UltraScale+ MPSoC (ZCU104) platform.
